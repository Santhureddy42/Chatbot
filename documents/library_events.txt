Riverside Community Library
Programs and Events

Regular Weekly and Monthly Programs

Community Reading Group
Tuesdays, 6:00-7:00 PM. Adults and older teens are welcome to discuss a selected book or share what they are reading. No library membership is needed to attend.

Children's Storytelling
Wednesdays, 10:30-11:00 AM. A welcoming story session for young children and their caregivers. Caregivers should remain with children during the program.

Riverside Book Club
The first Thursday of each month, 6:00-7:00 PM. The group discusses one book each month. Ask at the service desk for the current title and to reserve a place.

Educational Workshops
The second Saturday of each month, 11:00 AM-12:00 PM. Workshops cover practical learning topics such as research skills, digital basics, and creative writing. Check with staff because topics and registration requirements vary.

Practical Information
Events are held at Riverside Community Library, 100 Riverside Avenue, beside River Park. Regular programs are free. Some workshops have limited space and may require registration. Ask at the service desk about accessibility, the current book-club title, or changes to the event schedule. The library is open Monday-Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM; it is closed Sunday.
