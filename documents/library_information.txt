Riverside Community Library
Library Information

About the Library
Riverside Community Library is a neighborhood library serving Riverside and nearby communities. It opened in 1985 as a small reading room and has grown into a welcoming place to read, study, use public computers, and join community programs.

Location and Hours
The library is at 100 Riverside Avenue, beside River Park in downtown Riverside.
Monday-Friday: 9:00 AM-6:00 PM
Saturday: 10:00 AM-2:00 PM
Sunday: Closed
Hours may change on public holidays. Call or check the library notice board before visiting on a holiday.

Facilities
Visitors can use the main lending collection, quiet study tables, free Wi-Fi, and public computers. The meeting room is used for scheduled library programs. Ask staff for help finding books or using library services.

Membership
Library membership is free for Riverside residents. Apply at the service desk with a photo ID and proof of current address, such as a recent bill or official letter. Children may join with a parent or guardian, who must provide their contact information. Visitors who live outside Riverside can ask staff about a visitor card.

Borrowing
Members may borrow up to five books at a time. Books are due three weeks after checkout. A book may be renewed once for another three weeks if no other member has placed a hold on it. Return books at the service desk or in the book-return slot outside the main entrance.

Services and Programs
The library provides free Wi-Fi, public computer access, reading groups, children's storytelling sessions, a monthly book club, and educational workshops. Black-and-white printing is available for $0.10 per page. Ask staff about current program schedules and any accessibility needs.

Contact
For membership, program, or access questions, speak with library staff at the service desk during opening hours.
