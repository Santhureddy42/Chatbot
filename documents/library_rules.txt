Riverside Community Library
Membership and Library Rules

Membership Rules
Membership is free for Riverside residents. Apply at the service desk with a photo ID and proof of current address. A parent or guardian must register children and provide contact details. Keep your library card safe and tell staff if it is lost. Members are responsible for materials borrowed on their card.

Borrowing Rules
Members may borrow up to five books at a time. The loan period is three weeks from the checkout date. Books may be renewed once for an additional three weeks when no hold has been placed by another member. Ask at the service desk or use the library's approved renewal process before the due date.

Returns and Late Fees
Return books by the due date shown at checkout. Books can be returned at the service desk or through the book-return slot outside the main entrance. The late fee for an overdue book is $0.25 per book per day, up to a maximum of $5.00 for each book. Contact staff if a book is lost or damaged so they can explain the next steps.

Library Behavior
Be considerate of other visitors. Keep conversations quiet in reading and study areas, supervise children, and follow staff directions. Food and covered drinks are permitted only where posted; keep all areas clean. Do not damage library books, furniture, or equipment.

Wi-Fi and Computer Use
Wi-Fi is free for visitors. Ask the service desk for connection instructions. Public computers are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Members may use a computer for up to 60 minutes per day; ask staff whether a session can be extended when no one is waiting. Sign in as directed, save your work to your own device, and sign out when finished. Do not install software, change computer settings, or access another person's files. Follow staff instructions and applicable laws when using library technology.

Programs
Arrive on time for events and follow the host's directions. Some programs may have limited space and require advance registration. Ask library staff how to reserve a place.
